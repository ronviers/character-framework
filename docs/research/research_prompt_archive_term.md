# Research prompt — the ARCHIVE-HELD term (the deepest blocker to core)

**For the outbound multi-model research channel.** Self-contained; assumes no prior context. **Constraint:
open-access / freely-downloadable sources only** (no paywalled articles — we cannot buy them; prefer
open-access journals, author/lab sites, PMC, arXiv/bioRxiv, BioModels, public repos). Return a unified,
source-cited report: a **ranked shortlist of named substrates** for which the archive-held part of
organization is **nonzero, separable, and quantifiable from accessible data.**

## The object, and exactly what's missing

We are building a decomposition of biological/physical organization:

> **Organization = circulation-held `K(C)` + archive-held.**

- **circulation-held `K(C)`** = the organization a running non-equilibrium steady state (NESS) recovers after
  **complete turnover of its parts**, with no static record beyond replacement of generic components. We
  measure it as `K(C) = K_topo (= b₁, the protected cycle count) + K_metric (slow-manifold dimension)`. We
  have **two clean anchors**: KaiABC (`K=2`) and the peroxidase-oxidase reaction (`K=4`).
- **archive-held** = the distinctions whose fidelity requirement **exceeds** the circulation's self-specifying
  capacity — the standing high-dimensional part-spec the running fluxes **cannot regenerate** (DNA, on this
  reading: not "a bootstrap file" but the part-spec the dynamics *consult* but cannot reconstruct from their
  own running state).

**The gap:** both our anchors sit at **archive ≈ 0** (a chemical clock, a chemical oscillator — pure
circulation). So the **second term of the decomposition has never been measured**, and the capacity *curve*
(archive-bits required vs. maintained organizational complexity) has no point off the archive=0 axis. This is
the single thing blocking the decomposition from becoming a core claim. **We need a substrate where the
archive-held part is large enough to measure, and where the split from the circulation-held part is
well-posed.**

## What we need from a candidate (the affordance gate)

1. **A genuine maintained NESS** (metabolic/regulatory fluxes, an oscillation, or steady turnover) with
   **operator-buildable, measured/fitted kinetics** from an **open-access** source — so the circulation-held
   `K(C)` can be computed as we did for the anchors.
2. **A genuine archive in the loop** — a static record the running dynamics *read* (gene sequences, a tape, a
   fixed high-dimensional parameter set set by structure) that the circulation **cannot regenerate from its
   own state**. The archive must be **quantifiable** (e.g. genome information content in bits, number of
   distinct gene products, the dimension of the structure-fixed parameter set).
3. **Separability (the kill-gate — state it explicitly).** Can "circulation-held" vs "archive-held" be drawn
   **choice-independently**? Operational test: *scramble the parts at fixed record* → the circulation-held
   organization recovers; *erase/perturb the record* → the archive-held distinctions are lost and do not
   regenerate. If transcription/translation couples them so tightly that the cut cannot be drawn without
   arbitrary choices, **say so** — a substrate where the cut *blurs* is an informative (kill) result, not a
   disqualifier, but flag it.
4. **A point off archive=0.** The candidate should plausibly land at **archive-held > 0** with a maintained
   circulation — so it gives a genuinely new region of the (K_circulation, archive-bits) plane.

## Nominated candidates (each owes an open-access source with usable kinetics + a measurable archive)

1. **Minimal cell — JCVI-Syn3A** *(prime target).* The genome (~543 kbp, ~493 genes) is a **measurable
   archive**; the metabolic/regulatory fluxes are the circulation. Open-access data exist: **Breuer et al.
   2019, *eLife* 8:e36842** (whole-cell kinetic metabolic model — operator-buildable) and the
   **Luthey-Schulten lab "Minimal_Cell" public repository**. Ideal: archive (genome bits) and circulation
   (metabolic NESS) are both quantifiable, and the genome reduction is itself a probe of *which organization is
   circulation-held vs archive-required*.
2. **Gene-regulatory oscillators with explicit transcription** — the **repressilator** (Elowitz–Leibler 2000,
   the synthetic 3-gene ring; we already have a kinetic build) or the **embryonic cell-cycle** (Cyclin/CDK/APC;
   the design-space paper + supplements are already in hand at `docs/sources/second_capacity_substrate/`). Here the
   **gene identities / wiring are archive-held** (read from DNA, not regenerated by the protein oscillation),
   the concentration oscillation is circulation-held. Cleanest place to test whether the cut is even well-posed
   when a tape is in the loop.
3. **Ribosome / translation kinetics** — the mRNA tape (archive) read by a running translation NESS
   (circulation); measured elongation/initiation rates exist. The archive (codon sequence) is sharply distinct
   from the circulation.
4. **Any open-access system** with both (i) a measured-kinetics NESS and (ii) a quantifiable static record the
   dynamics consult but cannot regenerate.

## What a PASS must deliver (per candidate)

- **Full citation + open-access link + exactly where the kinetic constants live** (BioModels ID, repo path, SI
  table, fitted figure). Flag anything paywalled — we cannot use it.
- **The circulation side:** can `K(C)` (a NESS with cycle structure / slow manifold) be built from the data?
- **The archive side:** what is the record, and how is its content **quantified** (genome bits, # gene
  products, parameter-set dimension)?
- **Separability verdict:** is the circulation/archive cut choice-independent (the scramble-parts /
  perturb-record test)? Or does it blur — and if so, how?
- **Expected placement:** roughly where on the (K_circulation, archive-bits) plane — i.e. is it genuinely a
  point with **archive > 0**?
- **VERDICT: PASS / WEAK / FAIL**, with the single requirement that fails.

Rank by: (i) **measurable archive > 0** with the cut well-posed, (ii) open-access operator-buildable kinetics,
(iii) how cleanly it extends beyond our two archive≈0 anchors. **Name the single best lead and the exact
open-access data we would download to build it.**
