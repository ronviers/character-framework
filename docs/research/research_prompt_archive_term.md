## The object, and exactly what's missing

We are building a decomposition of biological/physical organization:

> **Organization = circulation-held `K(C)` + archive-held.**

- **circulation-held `K(C)`** = the organization a running non-equilibrium steady state (NESS) recovers after
  **complete turnover of its parts**, with no static record beyond replacement of generic components. We
  measure it as `K(C) = K_topo (= b₁, the protected cycle count) + K_metric (slow-manifold dimension)`. We
  have **two clean anchors**: KaiABC (`K=2`) and the peroxidase-oxidase reaction (`K=4`).
- **archive-held** = the distinctions whose fidelity requirement **exceeds** the circulation's self-specifying
  capacity — the standing high-dimensional part-spec the running fluxes **cannot regenerate** (DNA, on this
  reading: not "a bootstrap file" but the part-spec the dynamics *consult* but cannot reconstruct from their
  own running state).

**The gap:** both our anchors sit at **archive ≈ 0** (a chemical clock, a chemical oscillator — pure
circulation). So the **second term of the decomposition has never been measured**, and the capacity *curve*
(archive-bits required vs. maintained organizational complexity) has no point off the archive=0 axis. This is
the single thing blocking the decomposition from becoming a core claim. **We need a substrate where the
archive-held part is large enough to measure, and where the split from the circulation-held part is
well-posed.**
